Cook is a set of apps for [Cooklang](https://cooklang.org) recipes, the open, plain-text recipe format. Your recipes stay as plain files you own. The apps help you cook from them, shop for them and plan around them.

**Apps**
- [Cook for iOS](https://github.com/cook-md/ios-app): cooking mode, timers, shopping lists by aisle, recipe clipping
- [Cook for Android](https://github.com/cook-md/android-app): the same, on Android
- [Cook Editor](https://github.com/cook-md/editor): a free desktop editor for macOS, Windows and Linux
- [Sync Agent](https://github.com/cook-md/sync-agent): keeps a recipe folder on your computer in sync
- [Cook MCP](https://github.com/cook-md/cook-mcp): an open-source MCP server that lets Claude, Codex, Cursor and other AI agents work with your Cooklang recipes

**[Cook Cloud](https://cook.md/)** syncs your recipe folder across your phone, desktop and [CookCLI](https://cooklang.org/cli/). Paid plans fund the open-source Cooklang project.
